Your money, always in sight.

Pocket keeps your balance in the Mac menubar and tells you how long it will last. Your money stays on your Mac.

Download for Mac Free · macOS 14 or later · Apple Silicon and Intel
Cha-ching!

Know when it runs out.

Add your bills, subscriptions and paychecks once. Pocket logs them on their day and draws where your balance is heading, a month or a year out.

  • “Lasts past Jan 10”, or “Runs out on Dec 4”, at a glance
  • Every payment coming up, with the balance after it
  • Pause, skip or stop anything that repeats

Made for money that doesn't arrive on schedule.

Freelance pay, refunds, the friend who owes you: mark it as something you're waiting for. When it lands, click “Got it” and type what actually arrived, even if it came late or smaller.

  • A reminder on the day it's due
  • Quick-add buttons for the things you buy often, ⌘1 to ⌘9
  • Categories and a monthly breakdown of where it went
Can't wait!

Your money never leaves your Mac.

There's no account to make and no server holding your numbers. Everything lives in one file on your Mac, with a daily backup. You can export it, move it, or erase it any time.
